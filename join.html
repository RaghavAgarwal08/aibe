    Questions

    Things people ask.

    Do I need to code?

    No. Discovery, pricing, marketing, analytics and operations involve none, and the parts that do are taught with tools that don't require it.

    Do I need a business background?

    Also no. If you can build, you're useful here on day one — the business side is what we'll teach you.

    Do I need an idea?

    No. Each semester the board publishes a slate of projects. Join one, or get matched into a team.

    What does it cost?

    $5 a semester. The Treasurer waives it for anyone with financial need, and nobody is turned away from a meeting over it.

    How much time?

    A workshop is ninety minutes a week. A project team adds a few hours, more before demo day. Hackathons are a full Saturday.

    General vs Active Member?

    Register and attend one event to be a General Member. Ten meetings or one project team makes you Active — which lets you lead a team and apply for officer roles.

    Can I join mid-semester?

    Yes. Workshops stand alone and teams can take new people.

    Who owns what I build?

    You do. AIBE takes no ownership, equity or revenue share. If your team continues after the semester, we'll push you to write a founder agreement early.

    Are freshmen welcome?

    Very. Starting something in your first year and having it not work is close to a free option.

    Can I leave?

    Any time, without retribution or harassment. That's in the constitution too.

    Leadership

    Officers are selected by open application each spring — no elections, same criteria for everyone, every eligible applicant interviewed.
