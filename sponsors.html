    Partner with AIBE

    Fund the room where Illinois students build with AI.

    Sponsorship buys compute, food and prize money — and puts you in the room while they build.

    Start a conversation Support levels

    The case

    Most student sponsorships buy a logo. This one buys a Saturday with the students who build.

    Our members ship something, find out whether a stranger wants it, price it, and defend it to judges — before they graduate.

    We're new: chartered 2026, first full cohort Spring 2027. Early partners get far more visibility per dollar than they will later.

    Worth saying up front

    What sponsorship doesn't buy.

    Commitments from our constitution, not boilerplate.

    No endorsement

    We'll name you and give you a platform. We won't tell members your tool is the best one.

    No claim on members' work

    What students build belongs to them. We can't sign that away.

    No University endorsement

    AIBE is a separate legal entity and can't extend the University's tax exemption.

    Funds go to the club

    Never to an individual member's venture, including a prize winner's.

    Recruiting is opt-in

    We share a resume book of people who choose to be in it, not a membership list.
