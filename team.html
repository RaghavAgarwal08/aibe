    Faculty advisor

    Frank Liu

    Associate Professor of Marketing, Gies College of Business, University of Illinois Urbana–Champaign.

    Professor Liu meets regularly with AIBE's officers, attends board and general meetings as schedule allows, helps orient new officers, and advises on training, programme planning and the responsible use of AI in business.

    Per our constitution, the advisor attends board meetings as a non-voting member. Decision-making stays with the student officers.

    As AIBE grows

    Roles that don't exist yet.

    The Executive Board can create additional director positions as the club grows. These three are the ones we expect to need first. If one of them sounds like you, say so early — it is much easier to grow into a role you've already been doing informally.

    How officers are selected

    Proposed

    Director of Projects

    Owns the Project Slate and the teams: formation, milestones, demo day. Currently handled by the Vice President.

    Proposed

    Director of Partnerships

    Sponsors, Research Park companies, industry speakers and credits. The person who makes the hackathon budget real.

    Proposed

    Director of Events

    Rooms, food, logistics, field trips. The reason anything actually happens on time.

    Open now

    Newsletter editors

    A member team that reads widely and picks the week's best articles and papers. Not an officer role — just a good one.

    Mentors & advisory council

    Faculty, staff, alumni, graduate students and industry professionals who lead workshops, advise project teams and share what they know.

    If you've started something, run AI in production, invested in it, or researched it — and you'd give a few hours a semester to students who are trying — we would like to hear from you. Mentors participate in an advisory capacity and don't hold decision-making power in AIBE.
