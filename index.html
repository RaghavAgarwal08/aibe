    Why we exist

    Illinois has AI clubs. Illinois has startup clubs. This is where they meet.

    We treat AI as the thing that gets one student from a half-formed idea to a working product — inside a single semester.

    Every semester

    Four things you actually do.

    Weekly

    Workshops

    Ninety minutes, laptops open. You leave with something that didn't exist before.

    All semester

    A project team

    Your idea or one off the slate. Two to four people. Demo day at the end.

    Twice a semester

    Hackathons

    One day. Food, credits and compute. The winner gets funding.

    Monthly

    Founders & operators

    Alumni who built AI startups. Investors. Faculty. In the room, taking questions.

    The arc

    One semester, four moves.

    Weeks 1–2

    Turn up, meet people

    Kickoff, the semester's project slate, and a social so you know people before you pick a team.

    Weeks 2–14

    Learn by building

    A new skill every Thursday, taught so the CS major and the Gies major both leave able to use it.

    Weeks 3–14

    Build the thing

    Your team, your milestones. Two hackathons along the way to compress a month into a Saturday.

    Finals

    Show it to a room

    Demo day. Judges, mentors, members — and the semester's funding decision.
