    About

    A club for people who'd rather build the thing.

    Chartered at Illinois in 2026. First full cohort, Spring 2027.

    Why we started

    Two problems, one room.

    The builders

    Could ship, couldn't find a customer

    Get a model running, wire an agent, build a prototype in a weekend — then stall, because nobody ever taught us how to find out if a single human wanted it.

    The operators

    Could see it, were waiting for someone else

    Understood markets, customers, money. And were still waiting for a technical co-founder to make something real happen.

    What changed

    The wait is over

    One student with a laptop can now do the research, the prototype, the marketing and the numbers. The bottleneck isn't capability any more. It's judgment, taste, and actually starting.

    Mission

    A student-led community for turning what AI makes possible into real projects and ventures.

    What we believe

    Five things we'd defend.

    01

    Building beats reading about building

    A workshop that ends in a slide deck has failed.

    02

    The idea is yours

    Bring your own, or take one off the slate. Not just software.

    03

    Whoever builds it, owns it

    No equity, no revenue share, no financial interest. It's in the constitution.

    04

    Mixed teams win

    Someone who builds, someone who sells, someone who notices what the others missed.

    05

    Use these tools honestly

    Respect the terms, the privacy of others and academic integrity. Nobody records a meeting without asking.

    Where we fit

    Built to complement the clubs you're already in.

    vs. AI research clubs

    They go deep on the model. We start where a working tool needs a customer and a price.

    vs. startup clubs

    They teach the playbook. We assume a capable model is sitting next to you while you run it.

    vs. coursework

    No grade, no syllabus lag. Demo day is the deadline and your peers are the audience.
